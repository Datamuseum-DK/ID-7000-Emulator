=======================================
ID-7000 A ROM Reverse engineering notes
=======================================

.. note::

   These notes pertains to reverse engineering efforts for one ID-7000
   computer (originally stated to be lotated on pallet 422 in DDFs storage).
   Currently I have no further information on the provenance this ID-7000.

   The ROMs were located on what is named Board 5 in the bitarchive:
   https://datamuseum.dk/wiki/Bits:30007085

   For an overview of all ID-7000 artifacts:
   https://datamuseum.dk/wiki/Bits:Keyword/DDE/ID-7000
